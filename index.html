Finding Space Away From the Puck

Don't just watch the puck. Watch how the forwards maintain separation as the play develops and how open ice changes as defenders react.

WATCH FOR
  1. Where are all three forwards as the play develops?
  2. How does the weak-side forward create a passing option?
